## Search Surah jump stayed at the learner's level (likely cause)

`index.js` enables react-native-screens' `enableFreeze`, so the Map tab
is frozen and hidden while Search is on top. The jump params arrived as it
unfroze and the scroll, fired one frame later, probably reached a list not
yet laid out again, so it was dropped. `Map2Screen.tsx` now runs the jump
after `InteractionManager.runAfterInteractions` (held in a ref, cancelled
on unmount, not by the effect's own cleanup, which clearing the params
would trigger). `useMap2Jump.ts` also keeps a jump that arrives before
the map's first landing, which used to be overridden by the landing on the
active level. Not reproduced on a device.

## After a lesson, back to the surah just played

`useMap2Jump.ts` always scrolled to the active level (the backend's
recommended next) when a lesson ended. For a jumped-to surah that level is
back in the main progression, so the view ended at the bottom of the map.
`useMapData.ts` now records which surah the lesson belonged to
(`returnedFromSurahRef`, captured from `lastVisitedSurah` before it's
cleared); `Map2Screen.tsx`'s `returnSlot()` finds that surah's next
unfinished level from the map's own nodes (or its last level if all are
done) and the view lands there. The active level and Lumo are unchanged,
and the "back to my level" arrow is now worked out for where the view
lands rather than cleared.

## Nothing animates off screen; "back to the map" no longer stacks copies

User rule (MUST, 2026-10-22): no animation, timer or sensor runs while its
page isn't on screen. From the read-only animation audit:

- New `hooks/useScreenVisible.ts`: `useScreenVisible()` (focused AND app in
  foreground) and `useLottieWhileVisible(ref)` (plays/pauses a looping
  Lottie). `enableFreeze` doesn't stop native loops, JS timers, Lottie or
  sensors, so these are gated explicitly.
- Float loops now use the existing `useFocusedLoop`: `GuestGate.tsx`
  (guest Quests/Leaderboard), `StreakScreen.tsx`, and the lesson's
  `BismillahIntro`. Values now loop 0..1 and are interpolated to the old
  distances (-10, -8, -10), so they look the same.
- Gated on `useScreenVisible()`: `LoadingBar.tsx` (JS-driven),
  `LumoLoader.tsx`, `LoadingStatusText.tsx`, `HintButton.tsx`, the
  streak-repair snowflakes, and `QiblaCompass.tsx` (now uses the shared
  hook instead of its own copy).
- Lottie loops paused off screen: Streak flame, streak celebration, lesson
  summary medal, the "being built" book (`BuildingContent.tsx`).
- **`navigate('MainTabs')` -> `popTo('MainTabs')` at all 13 call sites**
  (ComingSoonPage x5, Search, Surah Progress, Streak, Streak Repair,
  Lesson x3, Lesson Summary). Verified in the installed routers 7.6.4
  (`StackRouter.tsx:385-396`): `navigate` only reuses the CURRENT route or
  one found with `pop: true`, so from any pushed screen it added a second
  MainTabs with a brand-new map, leaving the old tabs and the pushed
  screens (and their loops) mounted underneath. `popTo` goes back to the
  existing one; nested `{ screen, params }` still switch tab and deliver
  params (`useNavigationBuilder.tsx:715-746`).
- **Likely the real cause of the Search jump bug:** Search -> Jump pushed a
  NEW map, which showed its loading screen; the jump params landed while it
  was still loading, and its first landing always went to the learner's
  level. With `popTo` the existing, loaded map receives the jump. Explained
  from the code, not yet seen on a device.
- Left as is, to discuss: `replace('MainTabs')` after sign-in/onboarding
  (Login, SignUp, OnboardPath, Splash) can also leave an older MainTabs
  underneath (e.g. guest on the map -> Sign Up). Its loops are now stopped
  by the visibility gating, but the copy stays in memory.

## Tab bar sat ~15pt higher on every tab except Home

Cause: one component (`Map2BottomBar`), two different heights. Map2Screen
gave it 55% of the iOS home-indicator inset; `Map2TabBar.tsx` (every other
tab) and `ComingSoonPage.tsx` were switched to 100% on 2026-10-21 to fix a
black strip under the bar. On a 34pt-inset iPhone that is 19pt vs 34pt,
so the bar and its icons jumped up ~15pt off Home.

Fix: one exported `bottomBarClearance()` in `Map2BottomBar.tsx`, used by
all three. A light fill painted below the bar's own box in `Map2TabBar.tsx`
is a safety net only.

The 10-21 explanation for the black strip doesn't hold up. The tab bar is
the last child of the tab navigator, and nothing (no SafeAreaView, no
padding in App.tsx, RootNavigator or MainTabs) sits below it, so its box
always reaches the screen bottom whatever the clearance. A shorter bar
only makes the screen above it taller, and inside the bar the light
home-indicator strip grows with the box, so the full-inset change
couldn't have closed a gap. The likely real cause: commit 9438d39 (10-21)
backed the whole bar box with each screen's colour, dark green
`#0D3B26` on Quests, and that showed through the seams between the bar
art and the bottom strip and through the 1pt sliver at the right edge. The
light backing under the corners (commit 5304d44, "Quests bar hairline")
is what actually covered that. Not confirmed on a device.
