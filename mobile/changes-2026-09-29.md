# Changes 2026-09-29

## Fixed: map mountain hidden behind the sky (`MapScreen.tsx`)

**Report:** on-device, the mountain range looked cut off by a flat horizontal
line, as if the blue sky sat in front of it hiding its body; clouds sat over
the peaks instead of on the sky.

**Root cause:** since 2026-08-03 the mountain's top edge was tied to
`hudHeight` (measured height of the streak/XP/search overlay) and clipped
there. It was invisible while the HUD was short, but every time the search
button grew (64 -> 96px on 09-22, 2x on 09-29) the HUD got taller and more of
the mountain was sliced off with a flat line. `SKY_CLOUDS` also rendered after
the mountain in JSX, so clouds drew over the peaks.

**Fix:**
- Mountain no longer depends on `hudHeight`. It renders at
  `mountains_crop.png`'s own 1400x385 aspect (`MOUNTAIN_H = MAP_W * 385/1400`),
  bottom-anchored at `SKY_BOUNDARY_Y + sc(28)` so the grass still overlaps its
  base. Full silhouette always visible.
- `opacity: 0.9` removed from the mountain (sky was bleeding through the rock).
- `SKY_CLOUDS` moved to render before the mountain, so they sit on the sky.
- `hudHeight` state and the HUD `onLayout` removed (no longer used).

## Changed: search button (`MapScreen.tsx`)

- 2x its previous size (`streakPillWidth * 2`), per request.
- Centered exactly under the streak pill via a pill-width wrapper with
  `alignItems: 'center'`; the streak pill's own position is unchanged.

## Verified

`tsc --noEmit` clean. Release APK (x86_64) built and checked on the Pixel_5
emulator: full mountain silhouette, no cut line, clouds behind peaks, search
button centered under the pill. Not yet on a real device; not committed.
